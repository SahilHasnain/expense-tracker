Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the Expense Tracker app ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information. Using the app means you agree to the practices described below.

Data We Collect

The app is designed to be private by default. We collect the minimum amount of data needed to provide the service:

  • Expense and transaction records you enter manually (amounts, categories, dates, and optional notes).
  • Device information such as operating system version and app version, used only for troubleshooting and improving compatibility.

We do not require an account to use the app, and we do not collect your name, email address, or location.

How We Use Your Data

  • To store and display your expense records so you can track your spending.
  • To generate summaries, charts, and insights from the data you provide.
  • To improve app performance, reliability, and user experience.

Storage and Security

Your expense data is stored locally on your device whenever the app operates offline. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share data when required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users and the public.

Third-Party Services

The app may integrate with third-party services (such as analytics or push notification providers). These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

  • You can delete any expense record at any time within the app.
  • You can clear all app data through your device's settings.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the email associated with our app's support channel.
