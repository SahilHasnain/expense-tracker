Agar hum **2026** me Expense Tracker bana rahe hain, to sabse pehle ek rule follow karte hain:

> **"Don't build an expense tracker. Build the fastest way to know where your money is going."**

Har expense app ye features deta hai:

* Categories
* Charts
* Budget
* Reports

Ye commodity ban chuke hain.

---

# MVP Vision

**App Name (temporary):** Money OS

Main agar sach me market me launch kar raha hota, to ek aur angle leta.

Tum events dikhao.

Example:

```

-$120

+$42,000

```

Ye banking apps jaisa feel deta hai. Users naturally timeline samajhte hain, isliye onboarding bhi easy hoti hai.

---

## Agar ye mera startup hota

Main **version 1** me sirf 6 cheezein banata:

1. Ultra-fast expense entry (core feature)
2. Income + expense timeline
3. Monthly budget
4. Smart categories
5. Search
6. Cloud backup

Bas.

Agar user ye 6 features roz use karta hai, tabhi version 2 me reports, recurring expenses aur baaki advanced features add karta. **MVP ka goal feature-complete hona nahi, daily-use-worthy hona hai.**
